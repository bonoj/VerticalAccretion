# Vertical Accretion — Semantic Surface

> **Status:** Present-tense interpretation of the accepted executable on `main`.
>
> This document describes what the current simulation has earned semantically. It is not a design contract, roadmap, implementation specification, or inventory of everything the repository has ever explored.

## Authority

`index.html` is the accepted executable head and governs whether a capability actually exists.

This document governs the repository's present interpretation of what those implemented capabilities mean. It may name stable distinctions that are distributed across code, behavior, embedded semantics, and executed evidence, but it cannot manufacture executable capability.

Older design documents, experiment logs, candidate artifacts, and superseded specifications are evidence of lineage. They do not override the accepted executable or activate machinery that the executable does not contain.

When this document and executable behavior materially disagree, the executable is evidence that this interpretation needs revision.

## Semantic center

Vertical Accretion is a deterministic embodied descent experiment.

Its subject is not a finished mine, settlement, or optimal route. Its subject is the accumulating physical history by which inaccessible nearby space becomes operationally reachable.

The active loop is:

**local physical evidence → embodied intention → physically reachable intervention → logistics → physical consequence → new local evidence**

Three distinctions organize the simulation:

**Topology is perception. World-space direction is intention. Physical entities are state.**

And:

**Coordination may choose an intervention. Logistics must realize it. Physics decides what happens.**

The current accepted simulation deliberately studies that loop with one active worker at a time. Multi-worker coordination is not required to make the present physical vocabulary meaningful.

## Embodied authority

The worker acts from a physical position in the world.

Local perception may reveal terrain, traversal, material, obstruction, or opportunity. Perception does not grant manipulation authority. Excavation, pickup, placement, and construction contact must be physically reachable from the worker's current supported position.

The current implementation represents this with a bounded working envelope. Its numerical dimensions are implementation parameters; the semantic commitment is **bounded embodied reach**.

Newly changed geometry does not become authority merely because the worker caused it. A newly exposed foothold must become physically traversable and be physically occupied before it can serve as a new working position.

World-space descent intention survives changes to disposable local topology. Graph edges and sampled footholds are representations of currently perceived access, not the worker's ultimate objective.

## Traversal and access

Natural terrain is first-class traversal infrastructure. Construction is not a prerequisite for movement.

Traversal is evidence of usable access. A path that exists abstractly but fails current physical checks is not usable access.

Constructed structure and traversable structure are distinct. A member can remain physically present and structurally supported while geology intersects its walking corridor. In that state the member persists, but it does not authorize locomotion through the obstruction.

If a blocked constructed route exposes an obstruction that is reachable from a legitimate working position, that obstruction can become ordinary local excavation evidence. Excavation may clear the route; the structure itself does not confer excavation range or traversal authority.

## Excavation

Excavation is local physical intervention, not remote terrain editing.

The worker cuts reachable nearby material to earn a landing, approach, or clearance. A desired distant corridor is not removed simply because it would solve the worker's route problem.

Terrain deformation persists. Excavation therefore changes the evidence available to later action.

Each successful cut currently produces representative physical spoil. The simulation does not claim calibrated mass conservation between removed geology and spoil. The semantic commitment is that excavation has persistent material consequence rather than disappearing into an abstract work counter.

## Material and cargo

Material identity matters.

Construction stock is finite and represented by persistent material entities. Consuming stock changes the world; failed work is not automatically refunded.

The worker has one authoritative cargo slot. Pickup and release are local physical actions. Cargo is carried as an entity rather than converted into an anonymous inventory count.

Spoil disposition is separate from excavation. Excavation produces material consequence; a disposition policy determines what the worker attempts to do with material already carried.

The current default can carry spoil to a locally evidenced cliff and release that same physical object into gravity. Returning it toward home is an available fallback/policy. Disposal does not mean deletion.

## Construction

Construction exists to establish physical access that terrain alone does not provide.

The current vocabulary contains persistent full members and an intermediate staging apparatus. Construction consumes finite physical stock and depends on legitimate support/contact conditions.

Staging exists because bounded working reach can make the final structure impossible to erect directly from the current working position. A staging platform creates a real intermediate working surface. The worker must physically use that access; staging does not multiply tool reach.

Staging is not a general-purpose arbitrary cantilever grammar. It cannot independently justify unlimited extension. Full members likewise remain a deliberately coarse construction abstraction rather than a simulation of every post, brace, fastener, lifting operation, or erection sequence.

Support is consequential. Loss of required support can invalidate or fail construction. Failure persists as physical history rather than rolling the world back to a pre-construction state.

## Failure and local evidence

A failed local attempt is evidence about that attempt under the physical conditions in which it occurred.

It is not proof that the geology has no solution.

The worker can retain local evidence that an excavation or construction approach has exhausted its present usefulness. That evidence is tied to physical circumstances and can cease to apply when relevant material conditions change.

The exact matching and invalidation heuristic is implementation machinery. The semantic commitment is narrower: **local failure may prevent immediate repetition without becoming global authority over the world.**

Exhaustion of the worker's current repertoire means the current repertoire has no evidenced continuation from the current state. It does not prove global impossibility.

## Death, persistence, and succession

Physical failure can kill the active worker.

Death does not rewind the expedition. Failed structure, released material, debris, terrain changes, and corpse evidence can persist. Stock is not restored merely because the worker died.

A replacement worker can be created from the same worker factory at the entrance. Succession therefore separates persistent world history from the private state of the individual that produced it.

### Private evidence

Local exploratory and failure evidence belongs to the worker that experienced it. A replacement does not inherit the predecessor's complete search history.

### Demonstrated access

The simulation separately represents a demonstrated access route from the entrance.

This is shared operational evidence, not a destination, global path plan, visited set, or promise of future reachability. It records outward access that has actually been physically demonstrated under the route-memory rules of the executable.

A replacement may replay that route one traversal at a time. Every remembered step remains subordinate to current physics.

If remembered access no longer passes current physical validation, memory does not override the world. The invalid suffix is abandoned and the route retracts to the last physically supported demonstrated access.

Thus succession preserves some earned operational knowledge without granting the replacement the dead worker's private exploration.

## Determinism and evidence

The simulation advances through a deterministic fixed-step path. Seed, initial state, rules, and recorded interventions define repeatable experimental conditions.

Determinism is used as an epistemic tool: a physical or semantic claim can be challenged against replay rather than defended by impression alone.

The accepted executable carries an executed acceptance record across multiple deterministic seeds, including replay equality checks and targeted physical interventions. Those observations are evidence for the current vocabulary, not a guarantee that every seed is solvable or that every future extension preserves the same behavior.

Seed 741 remains an important regression condition because substantial vocabulary was earned against it. It is not the only legitimate world.

## Observer boundary

The observer and the worker do not have the same authority.

Camera movement, inspection overlays, seed selection, replay, exported records, deliberate worker killing, and support-removal probes are laboratory affordances. They exist to expose and test the simulation.

They are not evidence that the worker can perform those operations.

Observer intervention should remain legible as intervention so experimental causality can be reconstructed afterward.

## Current physical vocabulary

At the present accepted head, the simulation has executable evidence for:

- one active embodied worker, with same-factory replacement after death;
- local surveying and exploration of currently evidenced access;
- world-space descent intention over disposable perceived topology;
- bounded, support-dependent physical working reach;
- natural-ground traversal;
- local excavation that earns approaches, footholds, and route clearance;
- persistent terrain deformation and representative physical spoil;
- one-object cargo identity with local pickup, carrying, and release;
- independent spoil disposition, including physical cliff release and home fallback;
- finite persistent construction stock;
- persistent full members;
- finite-stock intermediate staging that creates a real working surface without extending reach;
- separate structural-survival and locomotion-clearance tests;
- support failure, debris, worker death, persistent corpse evidence, and replacement;
- worker-private local evidence;
- shared demonstrated-route evidence that remains subordinate to current physics;
- deterministic seeded replay and inspectable physical history;
- visible physical/runtime failure rather than silent recovery.

This vocabulary is intentionally smaller than the repository's historical ambitions.

## Non-claims

The accepted executable does **not** currently establish:

- multiple simultaneous workers or a concurrency protocol;
- collective planning, team commitment, atomic work claims, or shared project scheduling;
- a semantic model or LLM acting as physical authority;
- global exploration knowledge or omniscient path planning;
- proof that a stopped run is globally unsolvable;
- arbitrary bridge, cantilever, scaffold, or structural design;
- explicit lifting, hoisting, rope, crane, or post-deployment machinery;
- detailed stress analysis or general structural engineering;
- enclosed cave or volumetric tunnel navigation;
- voxel-scale hand excavation;
- calibrated mass-conserving geology and spoil;
- a general construction simulator;
- a claim that current numerical tolerances, stock counts, dimensions, or heuristics are permanent semantic invariants.

Historical documents may describe or propose some of these. Their presence in the repository does not make them current capability.
